# Receiving — Counting Books In

This is the wizard where you actually record what arrived. It's the final step of receiving, and it's where damaged copies get handled carefully, so it's worth reading this one closely.

The wizard moves through a few phases: you review the lines, confirm, and get a result you can save or print.

---

## The one rule that matters most

**"Received" always means undamaged copies only.**

The received count is what goes to the sales floor and to Shopify, so it must never include damaged books. Damaged copies are tracked separately and are *never* rolled into the received number.

The way you enter it mirrors how you actually unpack a box:

- **Qty arrived** = everything in the box for that line (good + damaged).
- **Qty damaged** = how many of those were damaged.
- **Received** is then worked out for you: arrived minus damaged.

So you count the total that showed up, then say how many were bad. The system does the subtraction. You never have to calculate the received number yourself — and that's on purpose, so damaged books can't accidentally end up counted as sellable.

---

## Recording damage

Each line can be expanded to a damage section. When some copies arrived damaged, you'll fill in three things:

### 1. How many were damaged
Enter the count. It can't be more than the number that arrived.

### 2. What you're doing with the damaged copies (disposal)
- **Donate / destroy** — the damaged copies are leaving; you're not sending them back.
- **Return (call tag)** — they're going back to the publisher via a call tag.

This choice is folded into the receipt notes so there's a record of what happened to them.

### 3. How the value gets made whole (resolution)
- **Credit** — the publisher credits your account for the damaged copies. The line **closes** at the received (undamaged) count. The credit covers the gap, so you're not waiting on more books.
- **Replacement incoming** — the publisher is reshipping on the **same PO number**. The line stays **partial** and open, because more books are still coming to complete it.

The difference matters: choose **Credit** when you're square with money, choose **Replacement incoming** when you're square with books. That choice decides whether the order line closes now or stays open waiting for the reship.

---

## Confirming

Before you submit, the wizard shows a summary — including the details of any damaged lines — so you can verify everything reads correctly. Once it looks right, confirm to apply.

The received (undamaged) quantities are what flow to inventory. Damaged counts, disposal method, and any notes are recorded alongside but kept out of the sellable count.

---

## After you submit

You'll get a result screen. From there you can download a receipt PDF for your records.

---

## A note on location

Books are received into the main location (HQ) by default. If you're receiving somewhere else, make sure the location is set correctly before you confirm.

---

## Worked example

A line ordered 10 copies. The box has 10, but 2 are water-damaged.

- **Qty arrived:** 10
- **Qty damaged:** 2
- **Received (worked out for you):** 8

You choose **Donate / destroy** for disposal and **Credit** for resolution. Result: 8 sellable copies go into inventory, the line closes, and the publisher credits you for the 2. If instead you expect the publisher to reship those 2, you'd pick **Replacement incoming**, and the line would stay partial until they arrive.

---

## Do's & Don'ts

**Do**
- Enter the **total** that arrived, then the damaged count. Let the wizard work out "received."
- Pick **Credit** vs **Replacement incoming** based on whether you're getting money back or more books.
- Check the confirm summary, especially the damaged lines, before submitting.

**Don't**
- Don't put the undamaged count in "Qty arrived." Arrived means everything in the box.
- Don't subtract damaged copies yourself — that's handled for you.
- Don't use **Replacement incoming** if you're being credited; that leaves the line open with nothing coming.

---

## Quick reference

- **Qty arrived** — every copy in the box for a line, good and damaged together.
- **Qty damaged** — how many of those arrived damaged.
- **Received** — undamaged copies only; worked out as arrived minus damaged. This is what sells.
- **Disposal** — what happens to the damaged copies: donate/destroy, or return via call tag.
- **Credit** — publisher credits the account; the line closes now.
- **Replacement incoming** — publisher reships on the same PO; the line stays partial until they arrive.
